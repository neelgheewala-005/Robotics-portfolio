## 💡 Troubleshooting

### Pages option not showing?
→ Go to Settings → Scroll down to find "Pages"

### It shows "No published content"?
→ Make sure you selected "Deploy from a branch" not "GitHub Actions"

### Still building after 5 minutes?
→ Try refreshing the page, or check for errors in Settings → Pages
