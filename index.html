Autonomous Line-Following Robot

Arduino Nano & AVR | May 2025

Arduino

WHAT

Design and construction of an autonomous line-following robot using Arduino Nano and CNY70 reflection sensors. Implementation of bang-bang and proportional (P) closed-loop controllers. Integration of interrupt-driven sensor processing with dynamic motor PWM control for real-time stability.

HOW

  • Engineered CNY70 reflective sensor array with 10-bit ADC conversion at 125 kHz sampling
  • Implemented dual control modes: bang-bang for coarse positioning and proportional P-control for fine trajectory tracking
  • Configured 16-bit Timer/Counter1 in Fast PWM mode (976.56 Hz) driving H-bridge motor channels via output compare registers OCR1A and OCR1B
  • Developed sensor calibration routine with EEPROM persistence for threshold values
  • Optimized interrupt service routines to minimize jitter and ensure deterministic motor response

WHY

Fundamental platform for understanding closed-loop control theory in embedded systems. Demonstrates real-time signal processing constraints and trade-offs. Proven track record of stable trajectory tracking (>95% success rate).
